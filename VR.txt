CRYSTAL TUMBLER - QUEST 2 / WIRED PC VR

1. Connect the Quest 2 over your Link cable and enter Quest / Horizon Link
   inside the headset. Keep the headset awake.
2. Start CrystalSlab.exe on the PC and press F10, or choose Quest / VR...
   and Enter VR. You can also launch CrystalSlab.exe --vr.
3. Look forward and press Y to place the cube in front of you if needed.

The app uses the installed Meta Quest / Horizon Link OpenXR runtime when
available. This choice affects only the app process; it does not change the
system's registered runtime. Check "Use system OpenXR runtime" before entering
VR to use SteamVR or another registered runtime. An explicit XR_RUNTIME_JSON
environment variable always takes precedence.

CONTROLS

Move/turn/lean: tracked in all six degrees of freedom. Both eyes use the
headset's actual poses, interpupillary distance and asymmetric field of view.
The cube is 1.04 meters across by default, 1.8 meters ahead of the initial eye
position, with its center slightly below eye level. No artificial locomotion.

  Trigger, either hand: aim at a crystal and remove it, with a short haptic tap.
  A: pause / resume physics.
  X: rebuild the source object.
  Y: recenter in front of your current position and horizontal heading.
  B: show / hide the floating controls panel.
  Trigger on panel: click or drag controls.
  Right thumbstick up/down while pointing at the panel: scroll.
  F10 on PC: enter / leave VR. Home on PC: recenter in VR.

The floating panel contains the existing object, color, material, lighting and
audio controls. Desktop keyboard controls still work. The desktop window shows
both eye images and settings; there is no additional ray-traced spectator view.

PERFORMANCE AND QUALITY

Start at 72 Hz in the Link PC app. In Quest / OpenXR settings:
  - Resolution: 35-100% of the runtime's recommended eye dimensions, default
    70%. Leave and re-enter VR after changing this setting.
  - Adaptive resolution: enabled. Adjusts the submitted eye subrects between
    50% and 100% of those dimensions, targeting 80% of the headset frame time.
    This reserves time for the compositor and Link; it is not a frame-rate
    guarantee. The live panel reports both eye resolution and full stereo GPU
    time, including geometry, caustics, tone mapping and mirror/panel copies.
  - Fast / Balanced / High: vary ray depth, sample count and photon count.
    Balanced is the default. Mirrors, refraction and tinted absorption remain.
  - Spectral dispersion in VR: optional, off initially. Enabling it traces
    separate RGB paths and costs roughly three times as many camera rays.
  - VR caustics: optional, enabled initially. Both eyes share the same photon
    lighting solution and geometry acceleration structures.
  - Physical cube size: adjustable from 0.5 to 1.6 meters across.

VR uses deterministic crystal optics and spatial antialiasing. It does not
reuse color history between eyes or across head movement. The desktop's
progressive/stochastic controls continue to apply to desktop mode.

Audio is stereo relative to your tracked head. Meta's headset output endpoint
is used when available. Other runtimes use the Windows default output; choose
the Quest headphones there if necessary. Existing saved audio levels are kept.

If the headset disconnects, loses tracking, or reports a session error, the
app stops submitting invalid views and shows connection status. Exit and retry
VR after restoring Link. Failed connections return to the desktop scene.

DIAGNOSTICS

  CrystalSlab.exe --vr-probe --settle 0
    Reports runtime/device/session initialization in CrystalSlab.log, then exits.
    Exit 0 means initialization succeeded; 2 means no usable headset/session.

  CrystalSlab.exe --stereo-test --capture C:\Temp\eyes --width 1400 --height 1488 --settle 0
    Runs 72 stereo frames on the PC without a headset. Writes eyes.left.png and
    eyes.right.png, and logs whole-stereo GPU time. This tests rendering, not
    a headset's latency, distortion, controllers or compositor.
    Add --validate-gpu for D3D12 resource validation (Windows Graphics Tools).

Official wired Link setup:
https://www.meta.com/help/quest/509273027107091/

Native C++20 + D3D12/DXR + OpenXR. Rendering and physics run on the PC. The Quest
receives the two eye images over Link. There is no browser, Android APK, or
standalone Quest renderer to install.
